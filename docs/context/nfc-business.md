# NFC Cards — B2B Business and Clients

B2B digital-marketing business in Mar del Plata, Argentina: NFC cards/plates and QR codes that help local businesses collect Google reviews, improve Google Maps / local SEO visibility and manage loyalty. The owner covers sales, design, technical production and delivery.

**Value proposition:** reduce friction for leaving a review. Industry benchmarks: 85–95 % of satisfied customers do not review unless it is made easy, and review recency weighs on Local Pack ranking. These are sector benchmarks, not the clients' own data. Success = signed clients, delivered physical products and measurable review growth.

## Hardware
Direct import from China validated for blank cards. Local distributor TANGOID SRL contacted. 50-unit pilot cost validated; 500 units under evaluation. Buy a single test unit before any large batch.

## Lessons learned
- NTAG213 is enough for reviews/redirects (stores only a short URL).
- At low volume, programming cost dominates unit cost.
- Realistic design outputs: SVG, EPS and PDF (CDR cannot be generated by code).
- The wkhtmltopdf + base64 chain for QR in PDF is unreliable.

## Method
Pilot first (50 units or one location) · in-person demo with the physical card, then PDF over WhatsApp · minimalist design using the client's real colors and photos · tools: Python (ReportLab), Node (qrcode, pptxgenjs), Inkscape, Sheets + Apps Script, n8n + SQLite/PostgreSQL, WhatsApp Business.
